AI Tool · 360° Photogrammetry

SSS360

Turns 360° video into clean photogrammetry plates for 3D Gaussian Splatting. It runs AI segmentation (SAM) on each equirectangular frame to automatically mask out skies, power lines, rigs and people, then reprojects the sphere into overlapping flat views and auto-packages them for RealityCapture — sizing each batch to your RAM and adapting quality to the source resolution (up to 8K).

Python PyTorch SAM CUDA RealityCapture OpenCV

AI Tool · Motion Capture

MocapOS

Full-body + hands motion capture from a single video — no suits, no markers. A portable, one-click pipeline built on GVHMR and HaMeR with a desktop GUI, turning ordinary footage into 3D body & finger animation ready to clean up in Blender or Maya. Designed, packaged and open-sourced end to end.
